Privacy Policy

Last updated: Wednesday 07/10/2026

DrDemic provides a school management platform used by schools in Tanzania to record marks, produce report cards and share results with parents. This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and how it is protected.

1. Our role

For student, parent and teacher records that a school enters or imports, the school decides why and how the data is used, and we process it on the school's behalf and on its instructions. For account security, subscription and payment records, platform monitoring and our referral rewards programme, we decide how the data is used.

2. Information we handle

  • School information: school name, motto, logo, contact phone and email, registration code.
  • Staff accounts: username, securely hashed password, role, class and subject assignments.
  • Student records: name, class and stream, student number, marks, grades, positions and teacher remarks.
  • Parent or guardian information: phone number and the username created for the parent portal, and which announcements have been read.
  • Payments: phone number used, amount, plan, transaction references and status. We never see or store mobile-money PINs.
  • Rewards programme: payout mobile-money number and withdrawal history.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, timestamps, login attempts, and security and error logs. Our logs are filtered so that passwords and access tokens are not stored.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service: marks entry, report cards, score sheets, rankings, analytics, announcements and the parent portal.
  • To create and secure accounts, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To confirm payments, activate access and process reward payouts.
  • To monitor performance, fix faults and provide support.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising.

4. Who can see data

  • Each school's staff can see only that school's data, and only what their role allows. A parent can see only their own child's results.
  • Service providers who help us run the platform (hosting, payment processing, email delivery) receive only what they need to do that work and are bound to protect it.
  • Authorities, where we are legally required to disclose information.

5. Children's data

Student records are supplied by schools, which are responsible for having the right to share them, including any permission needed from parents or guardians. We do not collect data directly from children and we do not market to them.

6. Security and backups

  • Connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Sessions expire, and are ended when a password is changed or reset.
  • Each school's data is logically separated from every other school's.
  • Login and sensitive actions are rate-limited, and access is controlled by role.
  • We take regular backups of our database to protect against data loss.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify the affected school or user and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Where data is stored

Data is hosted on cloud infrastructure operated by Amazon Web Services in Singapore. By using the platform, schools and users understand that their data is processed outside Tanzania under appropriate safeguards.

8. How long we keep data

  • School data is kept while the school's account is active. After closure, we delete or return it on request within [90] days, and backups expire within 7 days after that.
  • Registrations that fail verification are removed automatically.
  • Payment and reward records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations.
  • Login-attempt and rate-limit records are kept only briefly, and error logs for a limited period.

9. Your rights

Under applicable Tanzanian data protection law, including the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to certain processing, and complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission. Parents and staff should normally go through their school first, because the school controls its records. You may also contact us directly using the details below.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only what is needed to keep you signed in and to remember your language choice. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Pages load fonts from a third-party font service, which may receive your IP address.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will notify schools of significant changes.

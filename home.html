DrDemic School Management System

DrDemic is a modern school management system built for schools in Tanzania. It brings important school administration and academic workflows together in one web-based platform.

Schools can use DrDemic to manage students, teachers, classes, academic marks, results, report cards, parent access, payments and other day-to-day school operations.
